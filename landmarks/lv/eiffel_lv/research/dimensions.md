Shape facts: square plan turned 45 degrees to the compass (feet at N/E/S/W); four lattice legs to the first platform with
an arch on each face; a glazed two-storey restaurant box with a hipped roof on the first platform (the original has
none); four more legs to the second platform; a concave tapering lattice shaft; octagonal deck and domed cupola.
Color: painted tan/beige steel; dark glass on the restaurant. Night: whole lattice flood-lit warm gold, brightest at the
cupola and along the platform cornices; restaurant glass dark. A restaurant wordmark sits on the roof edge: not modeled.

Conflicts: platform heights from the photo (30 and 65 m) are higher than half of the original's (28.5 and 57.5 m); the
photo was used since the replica is not an exact half-scale copy. Not found published: base width, platform heights.
